# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Usina Docs is committed to a respectful, inclusive and technically constructive community. Participation should help people create, share and teach knowledge without intimidation or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- give actionable, evidence-based feedback;
- respect privacy, authorship, licenses and attribution;
- disclose conflicts of interest when relevant;
- help maintain a safe environment for contributors and users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks, humiliation or deliberate intimidation;
- publication of private information without permission;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- plagiarism or intentional removal of attribution;
- repeated disruption of technical discussions;
- retaliation against good-faith reports.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in official project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or take other proportionate action when this code is violated.

Reports should be made privately through an official maintainer contact channel. Reports will be handled with discretion, considering the safety and privacy of everyone involved.
